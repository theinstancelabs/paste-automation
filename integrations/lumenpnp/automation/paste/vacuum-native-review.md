# Native N1 vacuum surface-probe review

2026-09-30. Software/API review; no actuation, motion or contact measurement. Review found that opposite-sign pressure deviations could be treated as quiet; the policy now aborts at the same Z for either declared polarity, including during confirmation. All nine offline vacuum-policy tests passed after that correction. This is a proposed bounded runtime design, not a commissioned probing operation.

## Established path and important API distinction

`automation/scripts/Verify_Current_Pocket_Pick.js` uses N1, the N045 tip and head actuator VAC1, with fresh vacuum baseline and bounded 0.05mm descent. Its historical Z8.0..4.6 bounds, feeder coordinates, sample thresholds and part-on limits belong to that pocket operation. They are not scrap-board calibration. Its `n.pick`, feeder association, generic safe-Z moves and error retraction must not be copied into a surface probe.

The inspected live-configuration snapshot identifies VAC1 as `ACT171086c0660cfc3b`, owned by the existing `GcodeDriver` `DRV16982438146c1dd4`. Its native read template selects I2C multiplexer channel1 with `M260 A112 B1 S1`, selects pressure MSB with `M260 A109 B6 S1`, then reads one decimal byte with `M261 A109 B1 S2`; regex extracts `data:(?<Value>.*)`. Boolean actuation uses `{True:M106 P1 S180}{False:M107 P1};` followed by `{True:M106}{False:M107};`. Recheck exact live templates/config hash immediately before implementation/use; no PWM change is implied.

Installed-JAR bytecode review found an integration hazard: `ReferenceActuator.actuate(boolean)` and `read()` perform configured coordination first. `AbstractActuator.coordinateWithMachine` calls `motionPlanner.waitForCompletion(null, WaitForStillstand)`. Current VAC1 before-actuate and before-read modes both request that coordination. Null head-mountable completion can inspect all rotary axes and wrap B720 with a global coordinate reset. Therefore even a supposedly stationary `VAC1.read()` is not suitable for unreviewed reuse here.

The narrower established-owner alternatives are `driver.actuate(vac1, boolean)` and `driver.actuatorRead(vac1)`. Their installed implementation substitutes the configured actuator template and uses the driver's existing command/response path; neither calls the motion planner. `actuatorRead(vac1)` delegates to the read overload and returns the matched Value. Require already-connected, homed/enabled, idle verified owner and explicit stillstand before either call; no competing serial and no raw custom command sequence. Direct driver actuation bypasses the actuator's cached last-value update as well as coordination, so record acknowledged command state separately and do not treat its stale UI/cache as physical truth.

A safe runtime must use the reviewed existing single-worker executor pattern, not public `machine.submit`/UiUtils task wrappers: those append null-head completion even on success and may flush pending motion after exceptions. Do not change coordination flags merely to make old helpers run. For explicit completion, use the audited top-camera context whose physical mapped controller axes are X/Y only; a partial raw-Z target can be issued through that same controller while avoiding nozzle rotation/tool logic. Confirm actual installed planner behavior, empty queue, zero directional offsets and no actuator interlocks before admitting a Z-capable version.

## Coupled Z and collision envelope

The native mapping is N2 Z=63−rawZ; N1 Z follows rawZ. At fixed XY, decreasing rawZ to lower N1 raises N2 equally. If the right needle starts visibly clear of the bed/board, its clearance to those fixed objects increases during this descent. This does **not** establish unlimited travel: check the raised extruder body's clearance to its upper mechanical limit, motor/wiring/tubing and surrounding gantry. The nominal63 mapping is not a verified mechanical stroke.

N1 retraction increases rawZ and lowers N2. Bound a normal return to the exact observed starting rawZ; never issue generic `moveToSafeZ` or a larger lift. An unplatformed scrap may lift or slide once N1 seals to it: it needs stable support/restraint, and VAC1 must be released before a normal confirmed N1 retract. Sensor/serial/motion failure does not authorize blind cleanup motion or replay. Retain the fault record and use a separate stationary audit if completion is uncertain.

XY positioning must be independently cleared for both heads before descent. The right-head CAD offset is a nominal prior and the first camera survey confirms a lateral motion only; neither proves the coupon's surface Z or a lower probe floor.

## Concrete bounded runtime contract

1. Bind a fresh request to JVM, live configuration, exact raw/driver start coordinates, installed empty N1 tip, quarantined N2, target identity, stationary images, cleared XY path and full proposed coupled-Z envelope. Explicitly provide startZ, floorZ, maximum descent, step, speed and duration. Native rawZ is40steps/mm in the observed firmware; choose realizable increments and distinguish integer step targets from rounded M114 evidence.
2. Keep XY/A/B fixed; command only one rawZ increment at a time through the native planner, with narrow completion and independent five-axis readback. Snapshot model/driver coordinates before M114. Admit the driver's report precision (.02mm/.3degrees in the current reviewed survey policy) separately from exact commanded-target checks; do not repeat the survey's original sub-report-resolution comparison mistake.
3. At a stationary verified start, actuate VAC1 through the existing driver, settle, and collect fresh empty-nozzle samples. Supply an explicitly justified response polarity, baseline spread/noise bounds, candidate delta, consecutive-sample count and timing bounds. Historical N1 evidence supports a decreasing raw reading under vacuum restriction, but historical thresholds are not defaults for this setup. A clogged needle/nozzle or an already sealed starting condition must not be accepted as an empty baseline.
4. The offline policy requests no further descent at the first deviation outside the noise floor. Hold and confirm there. Sustained response is a seal candidate; partial/mixed response, baseline instability, timeout, invalid readings or reaching the floor terminate descent. Never seek a stronger seal by adding compression.
5. Save every sample, requested/acknowledged pose, elapsed time, actuator result and image. A pressure seal is not proof of hard contact: a compliant nozzle, near-surface flow restriction, contamination and board motion can affect onset. Establish a repeatable observed surface reference before converting seal candidates to Z calibration. Do not call `pick`, assign a part, modify nozzle-tip calibration or overwrite the paste profile automatically.

The existing `vacuum-probe-policy.cjs` is Node-only scaffolding (modern JavaScript and `Number.isFinite`); it cannot simply be evaluated by installed Nashorn. A small ES5-compatible adapter/port needs equivalence tests, including step acknowledgement and timing. The policy's exact `acknowledgeZMove` comparison must receive a precisely represented acknowledged target with separately checked readback, or be explicitly revised/tested for controller report precision. Driver read/command timeouts remain real blocking limits; timestamps/deadlines must be checked after each response, with no extra move following a delayed sample.

## Still needed before physical probing

- Current registered left-nozzle XY over an appropriate flat, nonporous, restrained coupon area, verified tip identity/emptiness, and visual baseline of both heads.
- Evidence-backed near-surface start and absolute descent floor, plus upper-travel clearance for the rising right assembly. CAD and camera observations can supply the nominal prior; no full manual ruler survey is inherently required.
- Fresh empty pressure stream and justified noise/polarity/candidate contract; archived successful pickups alone do not establish today's surface-contact height.
- Reviewed normal vacuum-release/retract sequence and separate fail-stop handling, followed by observed repeatability. No right-head extrusion is needed for this N1 reference step.
