# Bounded conditioned eight-pad FTP group

This optional scope extends the inline conditioning route to exactly four complete resistor pairs (eight unique pads), in explicitly reviewed pair order. It does not admit an arbitrary board job or change the existing two-pad or scrap scopes.

The target record has `scope: "ftp-eight-pad-commissioning-targets"`, four unique `pairReferences`, and eight `pads` in adjacent complete pairs. Each pad retains its registered, 0.01 mm quantized pose and explicitly authored `padAvailableReviewed: true` and `padIdentityReviewed: true`, plus `availabilityImageEvidence` and `availabilityReportEvidence` paths and SHA-256 values. `availabilityCapturedMs` is exactly the source camera report finish time and must remain within five minutes at validation. Native admission rehashes both files, verifies camera completion/session/configuration, and confirms that the image belongs to that report. The source timestamp cannot follow the target review. All original board, registration, revalidation, surface, tip-offset, current-image and both-head clearance evidence remains required.

`compensatedSequence.protocol` is `restore-dose-retract-lift-eight-pad`. The bounded choices are dose 4, 6, 12 or 20 degrees; restore/retract 2 or 3 degrees; forward dwell explicitly 200, 1000 or 2000 ms; retract dwell 500 ms; final relief 40 degrees. The final relief is exactly two +20 stages at clearance, with 2000 ms after the last. There is no inter-pair final relief. Every B stage must be accounted for by the exact conditioning prefix or one of the eight indexed pad sequences.

`inlineConditioning.protocol` is `scrap-condition-transit-eight-pad`; its exact experiment, prefix indexing, and actual 15-second retraction-to-first-restore limit match the two-pad inline contract. The recipe target surface is `scrap-conditioned-ftp-eight-pad`; request scopes are `contiguous-native-ftp-conditioned-eight-pad-preview` and `contiguous-native-ftp-conditioned-eight-pad`.

Only this scope admits up to 96 stages. Linear steps remain at most 10 mm XY and 5 mm Z with full native speed; B steps use the existing 0.05 fraction. One reservation charges the entire emitted B gross before movement. New group entries carry `batchScope: "contiguous-native-ftp-conditioned-eight-pad"`, permitting stage indices 0..95 with exact batch/request identity; historical untagged batch indices remain 0..39. Faults retain the full charge, verified B order remains mandatory, and replay is rejected. Cooperative stop permits only an already-reviewed clearance lift before stopping; it adds no B movement.

This is software admission, not a statement of deposited volume or physical qualification. Parent-reviewed current images and the selected dose remain necessary for each actual group. The observed R39 and R38 two-pad transfers differed in image size; their software completion does not establish stable volume.

The isolated offline helper is `prepare-conditioned-ftp-eight-pad.py`. It takes the same explicit `--experiment`, `--template`, `--barrier`, `--profile`, `--clearance-review`, `--image`, `--target-record`, `--previous-report`, `--ledger`, `--output` and `--xy-clearance-raw-z` inputs as the conditioned pair helper. It preserves authored reviews and pad order, computes indices and complete-route accounting, then invokes the existing disabled generic preparation gate. It does not query or dispatch the machine. The output target and recipe are new files; input records are not refreshed or overwritten.

The optional 1000 ms forward wait changes only each pad dose stage. Conditioning waits, 500 ms retraction waits, final 2000 ms idle wait, speeds, counts and gross accounting remain unchanged. This option does not establish improved physical transfer.

Dose 20 remains one existing −20 B stage per pad. The group-only cap is 96 stages; dose 12 uses exactly two −6 stages per pad with the selected wait only on the second. This admission does not establish deposited volume or physical qualification.

The 12-degree option adds eight stages versus a single-part dose. The complete route must still fit 96 stages; longer routes are rejected. No single −12 native command or stroke admission is added. Standard conditioning plus eight 12-degree pads charges 270 gross degrees.

Optional author input `retractDegrees` accepts integer 2 or 3, defaulting to 2. The selected value is copied into both the transfer conditioner and every FTP restore/retract. Retract wait stays 500 ms and final relief stays 40 degrees. A standard eight-pad 12-degree/R3 recipe charges 287 gross degrees with net −113.
