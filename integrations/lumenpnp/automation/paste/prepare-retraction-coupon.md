# Offline scrap retraction coupon recipe

`prepare-retraction-coupon.py` writes one recipe. It reads supplied files and runs the existing offline batch preparation gate in a temporary directory, then discards that temporary request. It does not read a camera, query OpenPnP, dispatch, alter a machine plan, or supply review attestations.

Provide `--experiment`, `--template`, `--barrier`, `--profile`, `--review`, `--image`, `--previous-report`, `--ledger`, and a new `--output` path. The template supplies the existing session/carryover identities; the barrier, reviewed image, provisional profile, latest completed report and current ledger must pass the existing preparation gates. Output creation is exclusive. Run the ordinary `prepare-contiguous-batch.py prepare` and native preview/finalization workflow separately before any reviewed physical execution.

The explicitly authored experiment JSON contains:

- `schema: 1`, `scope: "reviewed-scrap-retraction-coupon"`.
- `startRaw: {X,Y,Z,A,B}`, exactly equal to the supplied barrier; `workRawZ` equal to start Z and `clearanceRawZ` exactly 5 mm smaller.
- `targetsXY`: five distinct `{X,Y}` objects, ordered as the wipe endpoint, sacrificial conditioning point, and three test points. XY targets and work/clearance Z must lie on the 0.01 mm reporting grid. The wipe changes one XY axis by at most 2 mm; all later XY stages occur at clearance and each axis step is at most 10 mm.
- Optional `testWorkRawZ`: exactly three finite numeric heights, ordered with the three test targets; omission uses `workRawZ` for all three. Each must lie on the 0.01 mm grid, be greater than common `clearanceRawZ`, and be no greater than conditioning `workRawZ`. Conditioning and the initial wipe remain at `workRawZ`; every lift and later XY move retains the common clearance. For example, conditioning at 58.25 with clearance 53.25 permits `[58.0, 58.15, 58.25]`, preserving the 5 mm maximum Z stage. The authored experiment review covers these selected heights; each B-stage provisional gap is adjusted from the profile at conditioning height by `workRawZ - stageRawZ`. This does not establish a measured gap or authorize contact.
- `doseDegrees`: 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 or 20; `retractDegrees`: 2, 3, 4 or 6. Doses of 2, 3 and 4 use one existing allowed B stroke. Each test restores exactly the selected retraction amount before its dose; conditioning still uses 20 degrees.
- `primeDegrees`: 40 or 60, always split into existing -20-degree stages.
- Optional `preWipeReliefDegrees`: 2, 3, 4, 6 or 20; omission defaults to 20. Its 1000 ms wait remains unchanged. Fixed values: `idleReliefDegrees: 20`, `conditioningDoseDegrees: 20`.
- Optional `dwellMilliseconds`: 200, 500 or 2000; omission defaults to 2000. This controls the test deposit dwell and supplies the default conditioning dwell. Optional `conditioningDwellMilliseconds` independently selects 200, 500 or 2000 for the conditioning dose only. Prime, pre-wipe relief and final idle relief waits remain unchanged.

The authored review must hash-bind this exact experiment through `experimentEvidence: {path,sha256}`, and the supplied image through `imageEvidence`. It also supplies the existing reviewer, review time, clean-tip/prior-top image evidence and full raw-Z interval. The profile must hash-bind that same review through `measurementEvidence`; its gap estimate/uncertainty remain explicitly provisional. The builder does not select a surface, targets or physical gap.

The sequence is prime in two or three -20-degree stages, dwell only after the last prime stage, selected pre-wipe relief with 1-second dwell, named wipe, lift, one -20 conditioning deposit, then three test deposits. A 12-degree test dose is two consecutive -6-degree stages, with the selected forward-dose dwell only after the second; no 12-degree native stage is introduced. Each conditioning/test deposit dwells for its selected 200, 500 or 2000 ms, retracts by the selected amount, then lifts. Before each test deposit the previous retraction is restored. The final +20 idle relief occurs at clearance. B retains speed 0.05 and XY/Z retain 1.0 through the existing batch preparation policy; no firmware pressure advance is added.

Gross travel is `primeDegrees + preWipeReliefDegrees + 40 + 3*doseDegrees + 7*retractDegrees`. With the default 20-degree pre-wipe relief and a 40-degree prime this is 139 degrees for 6/3, 157 for 12/3, 181 for 20/3, 160 for 6/6 and 202 for 20/6. A 60-degree prime with 20/6 charges 222 gross degrees and uses 34 stages in the reference route. The 6/3 trial is a 50% amount ratio, 12/3 is 25%, and 20/3 is 15%. Gross and net motor travel do not establish delivered volume. All reserved gross still counts on a fault; current ledger/hash and runtime policy checks remain authoritative.

## Explicit preparation for transfer

Normal coupons omit `mode` (or set `mode: "coupon"`). For a separate scrap-only preparation that will precede a reviewed FTP pair, set `mode: "transfer-preparation"`, supply exactly two `targetsXY` entries (wipe endpoint, conditioning point), and provide integer `maximumTransferElapsedMilliseconds` in 1..300000. No `testWorkRawZ` is accepted in this mode. Other fixed fields remain required; `doseDegrees` and `idleReliefDegrees` do not add stages in this mode. The sequence ends immediately after the -20 conditioning dose, selected +R retraction and clearance lift. There are no test dots and no final +20 idle relief. Gross is `primeDegrees + preWipeReliefDegrees + 20 + retractDegrees`; net is `-primeDegrees + preWipeReliefDegrees - 20 + retractDegrees`. With prime40/default pre-wipe20/retract2 this is gross82 and net-38.

This mode neither authorizes a subsequent FTP run nor establishes pressure or flow. Preserve the completed report and an immutable copy of its exact verified ledger. A separately authored target record must bind those bytes, this reviewed experiment, a fresh tip observation, the elapsed limit and the explicit FTP sequence described in `ftp-compensated-two-pad.md`. If the interval expires or an extra B move occurs, reprepare on scrap. Do not subtract only R after a normal coupon that also applied +20 idle relief.

For the explicitly selected prime40, pre-wipe2, conditioner20 with 2000 ms dwell, test4 with 200 ms dwell and retract2 recipe, a normal coupon charges 108 gross degrees; preparation-only charges 64. These are motor totals, not measured flow. Changing the conditioning wait or relief does not establish pressure as the cause of transfer inconsistency.
