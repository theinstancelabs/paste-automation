# Proposed native left-vacuum approach adapter — no implementation authorization

This note specifies a future adapter around `vacuum-probe-policy.cjs`. It adds no executable motion route. Unknown surface geometry, collision clearance, sensor response and contact interpretation remain blockers. A vacuum change is a seal candidate, not proof of contact or substrate height.

The future request must bind a separately registered empty approach location, confirmed empty N1/no held part, quarantined N2, measured joint geometry and explicit swept clearance for both heads and attached hardware. The left approach uses decreasing raw Z; the right head rises by the opposite signed increment. Its reviewed minimum raw Z, maximum total descent, per-step size, speed, maximum duration and independent sensor thresholds must be measured or explicitly reviewed. No values are copied from a historical pickup or free-air baseline. Native soft limits and enabled flags must be read and enforced; legacy safe zones are not travel bounds.

Use the existing native single-worker ownership pattern and exact driver/JAR/configuration/axis identities. Bind a fresh position barrier, full raw/driver/native starting poses and fresh physical evidence. Reject queued or subordinate motion, directional backlash offsets, actuator interlocks, changed reader/command-list identity and existing faults. Require the installed error latch and full native response auditing. No generic nozzle movement, safe-Z helper, public task completion or second serial owner is permitted.

At the known initial clearance pose, acquire a fresh free-air baseline using the reviewed fixed VAC1 command templates. The sensor direction, noise allowance and candidate threshold must be explicitly reviewed from relevant measurements; absence of a plateau or opposite-sign deviation stops the policy. Execute exactly one policy-authorized partial raw Z target at a time with top-camera completion context. Preserve X/Y/A/B. After native stillstand, capture immutable snapshots and independently verify firmware position and coupled native Z displacement. Only then call `acknowledgeZMove` with the actual verified position. No sensor sample may be assigned to an unacknowledged target.

Each pressure read must use the existing driver's audited per-line command/ACK/response handling. Record every raw reading, timestamp, baseline, policy state, requested Z and verified Z before another decision. Any timeout, reset/error, unexpected response, missing position, timing violation, geometry violation or ownership loss ends the run. No next descent or automatic retry follows uncertainty. The policy's seal-candidate state stops movement and awaits interpretation.

Return and vacuum release need a separately reviewed contract before implementation. They cannot be inferred from a failed probe: automatic lift may lower the other head, and vacuum release can drop an attached object. On known successful completion an explicitly authorized retreat may retrace the verified approach corridor, with the same independent checks. On uncertain transport or position, issue no cleanup motion or blind actuation; retain the existing fault latch and require supervised recovery. This design must be reviewed against actual geometry before writing or enabling a runtime adapter.
